# Project cover sources

All covers are 1200 × 675 pixels. Downloaded source papers and video, plus generation intermediates, are kept outside the published site in the ignored `.local/cover-sources/` folder. Supplied original project figures remain in `images/`.

- **wafer-segmentation.webp:** An original AI-generated illustration, with typography composed for this website. This is not a KLA sample or actual segmentation output.
- **cirstag.webp:** Original logic-gate schematic and deterministic replot of the DES row in Table I of [CirSTAG](https://www.csl.cornell.edu/~zhiruz/pdfs/cirstag-dac2025.pdf). Values are mean relative changes in GNN-predicted arrival time: stable 0.0068%, sensitive 0.5211%, with 10× pin-capacitance scaling and 10% of nodes perturbed. The schematic is not the benchmark's physical circuit layout.
- **spectral-diffusion.webp:** Original conceptual method diagram. Noise and spectral curves are illustrative analytic geometry, not measurements or evaluated model samples.
- **spade.webp:** Handwritten-digit tiles cropped from the supplied `images/SPADE.png` project figure. The graph and connecting arrows are original method illustrations, not a newly computed ranking. [SPADE, ICML 2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/cheng21a.html).
- **sgm-pinn.webp:** Sampling diagram and layout created for this site, incorporating an adapted pressure-error panel from Figure 4 of **John Anticev, Ali Aghdaei, Wuxinlin Cheng, and Zhuo Feng, SGM-PINN: Sampling Graphical Models for Faster Training of Physics-Informed Neural Networks (2024)**. [Source paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.07358v1), [source figure](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.07358v1/results/Pressure_Vis_AR_1.0.png). Changes: crop to the SGM-S result, remove surrounding screenshot background, retain actual field colors and color ramp, re-typeset endpoint labels, and combine with an original sampling schematic. This adapted cover is provided under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), matching the source. The sampling dots are illustrative.
- **avatars.webp:** Actual frames at 0.0, 1.0, and 3.0 seconds from the previously linked [talking-avatar project demonstration](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HsqRwAYIS7WWpbNsqjjuBPfROzYh0opo/view), composed into an original filmstrip. The frames are generated output, not source portraits. The video has no audio track; no measured audio waveform is claimed.
- **vial-inspection.webp:** Actual negative and positive source examples from the supplied `images/lema.png`, recomposed with consistent framing. Original boxes and arrows are retained as source annotations, not new predictions or before-and-after results.
- **hair-detection.webp:** Original `images/hair.png` inspection image plus a magnified crop of the same strand (source coordinates 934, 384, 1283, 733). The original red annotation is retained; the layout connector is editorial. No generated sample or inferred mask.
- **salman.webp:** Original conceptual diagram of language-model input and output relationships, referring to [SALMAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18306). Text variants and highlighted relationships are illustrative, not measured model responses or scores.
- **sagman.webp:** Original conceptual diagram of graph perturbation and changed output relationships, referring to [SAGMAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08653). It uses no benchmark data or copied paper figure. SAGMAN is distinct from the older SAGRAM coreset prototype.
- **forecasting.webp:** Original conceptual timeline and calibration workflow. Past observations, specialist paths, and the widening forecast range are illustrative, without actual market data, claimed confidence levels, measured accuracy, or investment returns.
- **copper-dishing.webp:** Original conceptual cross-section and ML estimation workflow. Copper geometry and depth dimension are schematic, without measured profiles, numerical dimensions, or proprietary data.

The SGM cover's license applies to that adaptation, not to the rest of this website or unrelated assets. Source materials retain their respective rights. Local rebuild scripts are in `scripts/covers/`; they require Pillow, local source artifacts, and (for avatar frames) FFmpeg.
