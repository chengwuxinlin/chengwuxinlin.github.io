Graph learningBest Paper Nominee

The approach. Analyze input and output graph-based manifolds to identify sensitive circuit elements under changes in graph structure and node features.

ResearchPreprint · 2024

SAGMAN: graph-model stability

Spectral analysis of how graph neural networks transform relationships between nodes, revealing sensitivity to changes in edges and features.

Graph neural networksSpectral methods
Inside the work

The question. Which graph nodes become unstable when connections or features change?

The approach. Build distance-preserving graph-based manifolds with spectral embeddings and probabilistic graphical models, then analyze distortion between model input and output.

The status. Research preprint; first author. Applications include GNN stability, recommendation models, and robustness research.

ML systemsIndependent research · 2026

Forecasting with calibrated uncertainty

A point-in-time forecasting and model-comparison system that combines specialist predictions, calibrated probabilities, and tests of ranking stability.

Probabilistic forecastingModel evaluation
Inside the work

The question. How should models be compared when predictions arrive at different times and evidence is incomplete?

The approach. Split data by label-resolution date, combine specialist models with contextual log-opinion pooling, calibrate outputs, and compare matched forecasts with Bradley–Terry/Elo rankings.

The scope. Independent research using daily equity data, reproducible artifacts, and offline evaluation.

About the cover. A conceptual forecasting workflow. The drawn observations and forecast are illustrative, not measured predictions, performance results, or investment returns.
